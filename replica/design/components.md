# Components: reel counter (revision 2, offline Android)

Built from the component list in `../recon.md`. Platform: Jetpack Compose +
Material 3, themed by `android/app/src/main/java/app/tidemark/design/AppTheme.kt` with tokens from `tokens.json`
(generated by `gen.py` into `.../design/DesignTokens.kt`). Every colour below is a **role** (`accent`,
`sev-high`...), never a hex. Light and dark both pass WCAG AA: run
`python ../../.claude/skills/replica-design/contrast.py tokens.json`.

## Ground rules

- **Nothing from BrainPal.** No screenshots were available, so nothing was
  measured from it. This is our own system on standard Android and Material
  patterns. No "brain" artwork, no copy, no brand colour.
- **Icons:** Lucide (ISC licence), imported as vector drawables. One stroke
  width (2 px on a 24 grid), 24 dp default, 20 dp in dense rows. App icons of
  Instagram, TikTok and YouTube are shown only through the system
  (`PackageManager.getApplicationIcon`) and never shipped in the APK. Brand
  colours of those apps are never used.
- **Font:** Inter (SIL OFL 1.1), bundled in `res/font` so nothing is
  downloaded. Every number uses tabular figures (`tnum`) so counts don't
  jiggle as they change.
- **Illustrations:** empty states use a single slot. Commission or draw your
  own during `/replica-brand`; until then, a Lucide icon at 48 dp in
  `text-muted`.
- **Severity is never colour alone.** Every severity colour comes with a word
  ("Calm", "Rising", "High", "Over goal") or an icon, for colour-blind users
  and screen readers.
- **Touch targets** are at least 48 × 48 dp, even when the visual is smaller.
- **Font scale** works up to 200%. Layouts wrap; nothing truncates a number.
- **TalkBack:** every interactive element has a role and a label. Live
  numbers announce politely (`liveRegion = Polite`), at most once every 10
  videos, so TalkBack isn't spammed.
- **Reduced motion:** when the system's "Remove animations" is on, there is no
  count bump and no slide, and show/hide is instant.

## Severity scale (shared by meter, counter pill and goal card)

| level | when (with a goal) | when (no goal) | role | label |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| low | < warn_at_pct | < 50 videos today | `sev-low` | Calm |
| mid | ≥ warn_at_pct | 50-149 | `sev-mid` | Rising |
| high | ≥ 95% | 150-299 | `sev-high` | High |
| over | ≥ 100% | ≥ 300 | `sev-over` | Over goal / Very high |

The no-goal thresholds are placeholders to tune after testing; keep them in
one constant.

## Layout

- Phone, single column, 16dp gutter. On tablets and foldables, a centred
  600dp column.
- Top app bar 64dp (title type title). Bottom navigation 80dp. Edge-to-edge
  with insets handled.
- S05: ring meter, then a 2-column stat card grid, then per-app rows, then
  banners on top when needed.
- Spacing comes only from the scale (4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64).
  Section gap 24, card gap 12, inner padding 16.
